When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Will it reach the flag? If the steps inside never get there, the loop never stops.
  • Count, then until. Use a plain repeat for the part you can count.
  • One step of the pattern goes inside repeat until at flag.
  • Run, watch, fix. Watch where the rover gets stuck.
Ask: what would happen with a plain repeat if the road got longer?
