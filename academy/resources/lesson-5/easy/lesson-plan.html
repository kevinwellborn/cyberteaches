Lesson 5: Repeat until practice

Time 50 minutes
Unit Loops
Puzzles 5.1 to 5.10

Overview

A practice lesson with no new blocks. Students use repeat until at flag on roads in every direction, add a turn before the loop, and put short patterns (crystals, stairs) inside the loop.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Use repeat until at flag to reach a flag in any direction.
  2. Add a turn before a repeat-until loop when needed.
  3. Place one copy of a pattern inside a repeat-until loop.
  4. Find and fix bugs in short programs.

Materials

  • Projector and the Lesson 5 slides
  • Printed Lesson 5 classwork, one per student (answer key for the teacher)
  • Student devices with a web browser: cyberteaches.com/academy
  • A class on the Teacher Dashboard, with student logins or Google sign-in

Before class

  • Try puzzles 5.1 to 5.10 yourself (the answer key is on the Teacher Dashboard).
  • Plan a random moment to say "stop" in the warm-up.

Lesson procedure

TimeStageTeacher and student activity
5 minWarm-up
Until you hear stop Slide 3		Students march in place until the teacher says "stop". Discuss: you kept checking and stopped when it happened.
10 minDirect instruction
Slides 4 to 6		Review repeat until at flag. Show that the direction matters (puzzle 5.3, facing left) and that you can turn first (puzzle 5.4). Show count, then until (puzzle 5.7).
5 minGuided practice
Think, pair, share Slides 7 to 8		Solve puzzle 5.4 (turn around) together, then pairs find one step of the stairs in puzzle 5.8 before the reveal.
12 minUnplugged practice
Classwork Slide 10		Students complete Parts A to C and E in pairs. Part C is a debugging task. Part D is for early finishers.
15 minIndependent practice
Code Slide 11		Students solve puzzles 5.1 to 5.10. Encourage them to predict where the loop stops before pressing Run.
3 minClosure
Exit ticket Slide 12		Exit ticket: facing down, flag to the left. Answer: R until flag [ F ].

Assessment

  • Formative: classwork Parts A to C, observation during coding, and the exit ticket.
  • Progress: the Teacher Dashboard shows stars per puzzle and a Lesson 5 score out of 100 (3 stars = 100, 2 stars = 90, 1 star = 80 per puzzle).
  • Success criteria: the student solves at least 8 of 10 puzzles and fixes at least 3 of the 4 bugs in Part C.

Common misconceptions

  • Turning the wrong way when the rover faces down or left.
  • Leaving out collect inside the loop.
  • Thinking the loop needs a number.

Differentiation

  • Support: let students stand and face the rover's direction to choose the turn.
  • Extension: all 3 stars; explain to a partner why one program works for any distance.
  • Accessibility: blocks can be added by tapping or by dragging, and moved with the ▲▼ buttons.

Supports for English learners

  • Visuals: post the block pictures and vocabulary on a word wall; point to blocks while you say them.
  • Gestures: students stand and turn left or right with the rover before coding turns.
  • Partners: pair students to read programs aloud; first-language talk is welcome.
  • Sentence frames: “First the rover turns ___. Then it moves forward until the ___.” “The program has a bug. I ___ it.” “The loop ___ when the rover is on the flag.”
  • Classwork Part E uses a word bank, pictures and fill-in sentences.

Connections

  • Science: instruments that measure until a value is reached.
  • Everyday life: "wait until the light turns green."
