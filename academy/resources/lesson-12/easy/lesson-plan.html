Lesson 12: If / else practice

Time 50 minutes
Unit Conditionals
Puzzles 12.1 to 12.10

Overview

A practice lesson with no new blocks. Students choose the right condition for each map (path, crystal or tank), write the else part, and combine two separate if / else checks in one loop.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Choose a condition that matches the map (path, crystal, tank).
  2. Write both the if and else parts correctly.
  3. Use two if / else checks inside one repeat-until loop.
  4. Explain their choice of condition.

Assessment

  • Formative: classwork Parts A to C, observation during coding, and the exit ticket.
  • Progress: the Teacher Dashboard shows stars per puzzle and a Lesson 12 score out of 100 (3 stars = 100, 2 stars = 90, 1 star = 80 per puzzle).
  • Success criteria: the student solves at least 8 of 10 puzzles and justifies the condition they chose.

Common misconceptions

  • Choosing path ahead when the map has crystals or tanks to handle.
  • Leaving the else part empty when an action is needed.
  • Putting two actions in one if when they should be separate checks.

Differentiation

  • Support: give a condition card set (path, crystal, tank) to choose from.
  • Extension: 3 stars on 12.9 and 12.10; explain a nested decision to a partner.
  • Accessibility: blocks can be added by tapping or by dragging, and moved with the ▲▼ buttons.

Supports for English learners

  • Visuals: post the block pictures and vocabulary on a word wall; point to blocks while you say them.
  • Gestures: students stand and turn left or right with the rover before coding turns.
  • Partners: pair students to read programs aloud; first-language talk is welcome.
  • Sentence frames: “If there is a crystal here, the rover ___ it.” “If there is a tank ahead, the rover ___. Else it moves.” “The road turns left, so the else part is ___.”
  • Classwork Part E uses a word bank, pictures and fill-in sentences.

Connections

  • Math: even and odd, sorting by a rule.
  • Everyday life: choosing an action based on a sign or signal.
