Learning goals

  • Explain the difference between repeat and repeat until
  • Use repeat until at flag to reach a flag
  • Avoid a loop that never stops
Read the goals aloud. Students check them off with the exit ticket at the end.
Warm-up · 3 minutes

Walk until the wall

Instruction 1: "Take 6 steps."

Instruction 2: "Walk until you touch the wall."

Which one works from any starting spot?

Answer

"Walk until the wall" works from anywhere. You don't need to count.

Ask two students to start at different distances from the wall. "Take 6 steps" only works for one of them. "Walk until you touch the wall" works for both.

Watch out

A loop that never stops

If the blocks inside never get the rover to the flag, it repeats forever.

repeat until at flag
turn right

Turning on the spot never reaches the flag. This is an infinite loop.

The game stops infinite loops and shows "stuck in a loop". Ask: what is missing? (move forward).

When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Will it reach the flag? If the steps inside never get there, the loop never stops.
  • Count, then until. Use a plain repeat for the part you can count.
  • One step of the pattern goes inside repeat until at flag.
  • Run, watch, fix. Watch where the rover gets stuck.
Infinite loops are a normal bug. Celebrate students who find and fix one.

Before you go

Why is repeat until at flag better than repeat 9 times when you don't know how far the flag is?

It stops by itself at the flag, so it works for any distance. You don't need to count.
