Warm-up · 3 minutes

Security guard

A guard walks down a hallway. At every door they look. If the door is open, they close it.

What does the guard do at every door? When do they stop?

Patrol

Step, look, close if open. Stop when all doors are closed.

The guard routine is exactly a patrol program: a repeated step with an if inside.

When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Face it first. The shell flies the way the rover faces.
  • Shells are limited. Only fire when a tank is in your line of fire.
  • Red ! means danger. Fire or move out of the tank's line on your next move.
  • Run, watch, fix. Count tanks and shells before you run.
Remind students to turn back after looking, or the next move goes the wrong way.
