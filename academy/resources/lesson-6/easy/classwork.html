Part D and Reflect

Answers vary. Look for a correct start direction, a program a partner can follow, and a mix of repeat and repeat until. Reflection: use a plain repeat for the part you can count, and repeat until at flag for the part that goes all the way to the flag.

Exit ticket

It drives 2 squares and turns right, again and again, until it reaches the flag (a square path).
