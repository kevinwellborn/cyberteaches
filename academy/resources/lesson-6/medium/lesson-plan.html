Lesson 6: Repeat until patterns

Time 50 minutes
Unit Loops
Puzzles 6.1 to 6.10

Overview

A pattern lesson with no new blocks. Students put multi-step patterns and counting repeats inside repeat until at flag, and combine counted sections with a repeat-until section in one program.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Put a pattern that includes turns inside a repeat-until loop.
  2. Nest a counting repeat inside a repeat-until loop.
  3. Combine counted steps and a repeat-until loop in one program.
  4. Explain which part of a program is counted and which part runs until the flag.

Assessment

  • Formative: classwork Parts A to C, observation during coding, and the exit ticket.
  • Progress: the Teacher Dashboard shows stars per puzzle and a Lesson 6 score out of 100 (3 stars = 100, 2 stars = 90, 1 star = 80 per puzzle).
  • Success criteria: the student solves at least 7 of 10 puzzles and can identify the counted and the repeat-until parts of a program.

Common misconceptions

  • Putting the counted part inside the repeat-until loop.
  • Expecting the loop to stop in the middle of the pattern (it does stop as soon as the rover reaches the flag).
  • Off-by-one counts on long sides.

Differentiation

  • Support: have students trace with a finger and say each step aloud; provide the outer loop and let them fill in the inside.
  • Extension: try puzzle 6.9 (crystal U-turn) and 6.10 (rectangle to flag) and explain the two repeats to a partner.
  • Accessibility: blocks can be added by tapping or by dragging, and moved with the ▲▼ buttons.

Supports for English learners

  • Visuals: post the block pictures and vocabulary on a word wall; point to blocks while you say them.
  • Gestures: students stand and turn left or right with the rover before coding turns.
  • Partners: pair students to read programs aloud; first-language talk is welcome.
  • Sentence frames: “Inside the loop, the pattern is ___.” “I count the first part, ___ I use repeat until.” “The rover stops when it reaches the ___.”
  • Classwork Part E uses a word bank, pictures and fill-in sentences.

Connections

  • Math: perimeter of rectangles (long side, short side).
  • PE: laps and circuits that repeat until time is up.
