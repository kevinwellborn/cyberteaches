Warm-up · 3 minutes

Most important first

You are crossing the street. Which do you check first?

a) Is my shoe tied?   b) Is a car coming?

Answer

b) The car! Check the most important thing first.

The order of checks matters. For the rover, a tank is more important than a crystal or a path.
Big idea

The right-hand rule

Always take a right turn when you can. Else go straight. Else turn left.

until flag [ if path right [ R F ]
else [ if path ahead [ F ] else [ L ] ] ]

Puzzle 13.3. The right-hand rule finds the side road instead of driving into the dead end.
Big idea

Check the most important thing first

1. Tank ahead? → fire

2. Else, crystal here? → collect

3. Else, path ahead? → move. Else turn.

A tank blocks the path, so check it before the path.

This order is used in the final maze (13.10).

When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Read it as a sentence: "If there is a path ahead, move forward, else turn right."
  • Check at every step. Put the if / else inside repeat until at flag.
  • Most important first. Check for a tank before you check the path.
  • Left or right? Paths are checked from the rover's point of view.
