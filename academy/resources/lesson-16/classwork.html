Part A

1. After the loop: 3. After change -1: 2. It moves 1 + 2 + 2 = 5 squares, to the flag. The loop moves 1, then 2 squares and leaves 3 in the box. Taking 1 away leaves 2 for the last move.

2. It checks 4 times and collects 2 times. The check is inside repeat steps times, so it runs on all 4 squares, but only 2 of them have a crystal.

Part D and Reflect

Answers vary. Look for a correct start direction, a program a partner can follow, and checks every square. Reflection: inside, it runs on every square the rover drives over; after the loop it would only check the corner squares and miss crystals.

Exit ticket

change -2 (5 - 2 = 3), then the second loop changes by -1.
