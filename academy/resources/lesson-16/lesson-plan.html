Lesson 16: Variable challenges

Time 50 minutes
Unit Variables
Puzzles 16.1 to 16.10

Overview

The final variables lesson. Students combine the steps box with blocks from earlier lessons: repeat until at flag around a growing spiral, if / else checks for crystals (on every square) and tanks (at every corner), laps that use each length twice, and two loops with the variable fixed in between.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Put a variable loop inside repeat until at flag.
  2. Place an if / else check inside or after repeat steps times, depending on what it must check.
  3. Use change steps by between two loops to set up the next pattern.
  4. Plan a program in stages and test each stage.

Assessment

  • Formative: classwork Parts A to C, observation during coding, and the exit ticket.
  • Progress: the Teacher Dashboard shows stars per puzzle and a Lesson 16 score out of 100 (3 stars = 100, 2 stars = 90, 1 star = 80 per puzzle).
  • Success criteria: the student solves at least 7 of 10 puzzles and can explain where a crystal check and a tank check go.

Common misconceptions

  • Putting if crystal here after repeat steps times, so only corner squares are checked.
  • Forgetting that the box keeps its value after a loop ends.
  • Firing at every corner without if tank ahead and running out of shells.
  • Using repeat until at flag when a plain repeat count is needed for another part.

Differentiation

  • Support: let students build and test the plain spiral first, then add one check at a time; provide the "box card" from Lesson 14.
  • Extension: 3 stars on 16.8 and 16.10; design a spiral with crystals and tanks for a partner.
  • Accessibility: blocks can be added by tapping or by dragging, and moved with the ▲▼ buttons.

Supports for English learners

  • Visuals: post the block pictures and vocabulary on a word wall; point to blocks while you say them.
  • Gestures: students stand and turn left or right with the rover before coding turns.
  • Partners: pair students to read programs aloud; first-language talk is welcome.
  • Sentence frames: “I use repeat until at flag when I can not ___ the laps.” “The crystal check goes ___ repeat steps times.” “The box holds 5. I change it by -2 to make ___.”
  • Classwork Part E uses a word bank, pictures and fill-in sentences.

Connections

  • Math: patterns that grow then shrink (1, 2, 3, 2, 1) and finding a missing step.
  • Careers: game developers combine variables, loops and conditions to control characters.
