Warm-up · 3 minutes

Up and down

Clap 1, 2, 3, 4 times, then 3, 2, 1 times.

You add 1 after every round on the way up. After the 4 claps, the box holds 5. What do you change it by to start the way down at 3?

Answer

Change by -2 (5 - 2 = 3), then count down with -1.

This is exactly puzzle 16.5 (Up and down). Do it as a class with a number card for the box.

When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Can you count the laps? If not, put repeat until at flag around your loop.
  • Every square or every corner? A check for every square goes inside repeat steps times. A check for each corner goes after it.
  • Two loops, one box. After the first loop, ask what is in the box now. Change it before the next loop starts.
  • Shells are limited. Use if tank ahead so you only fire when a tank is there.
Encourage students to build in stages: get the plain spiral working, then add the crystal or tank check. Celebrate finishing the variables unit!
