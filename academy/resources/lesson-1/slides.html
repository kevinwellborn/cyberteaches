Warm-up · 3 minutes

Be the robot

Give a partner instructions to walk to the classroom door.

Rules: the robot only understands

step forward turn left turn right

The robot does exactly what you say, nothing more.

Think about it

Did you say "step forward" over and over? Is there a shorter way to say it?

Pick one pair to demo at the front. Count out loud how many times "step forward" was said. Ask: "Is there a shorter way?" Students usually say "take 5 steps". That is a repeat, which is today's big idea.
Big idea 1

What is a program?

A program is a list of instructions for a computer.

The computer runs them one at a time, from top to bottom. This is called a sequence.

Change the order, and you change what happens.

1, 2, 3… move forwardturn rightmove forwardcollect crystal
Key vocabulary: program, sequence. Ask: "What would happen if collect crystal came first?" (The rover would try to collect on an empty square.)

Big idea 2

Spot the pattern

move forward collect crystal move forward collect crystal move forward collect crystal move forward collect crystal

The same 2 blocks happen 4 times.

A pattern is a group of steps that happens again and again.

When you see a pattern, you can use a loop.

Vocabulary: pattern. Ask students to describe the pattern in words: "move forward, then collect, 4 times".
New block

The repeat block

repeat 4 times
move forwardcollect crystal

The blocks inside run again and again, as many times as you choose.

8 blocks → 3 blocks

The repeat block counts as one block, plus the blocks inside it.

A repeat block is a loop. Show how to count blocks: repeat (1) + move forward (1) + collect (1) = 3. In the app, tap the repeat block's top row, then tap blocks to put them inside. The ▲▼ buttons move a block in or out.

Why shorter?

Short programs are easier to read, easier to fix, and easier to change.

Stars also count toward ranks (Recruit, Private, Corporal…) and the class leaderboard. Encourage students to get it working first, then go back and make it shorter for 3 stars.

When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Run, watch, fix. Watch where the rover goes wrong, then change one thing.
  • Count first. Count the crystals before you set the repeat number.
  • Left or right? Turn from the rover's point of view.
  • Inside or outside? Check which blocks are inside the repeat.
Bugs are normal, and even professional programmers fix them every day. Encourage "change one thing at a time" so students learn what each change does.
