When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Face it first. The shell flies the way the rover faces.
  • Shells are limited. Only fire when a tank is in your line of fire.
  • Red ! means danger. Fire or move out of the tank's line on your next move.
  • Run, watch, fix. Count tanks and shells before you run.
Discuss the red ! warning: tanks fire if the rover stays in their line for another move.

Before you go

Why do we use if tank ahead before fire cannon in a patrol?

So the rover only fires when a tank is there and does not waste its limited shells.
