Part D and Reflect

Answers vary. Look for a correct start direction, a program a partner can follow, and the difference between two lengths. Reflection: subtract one length from the next: 2, 4, 6 jumps by 2, so change steps by +2. If the lengths get smaller, the change is a minus number.
