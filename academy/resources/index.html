Teach each lesson, then let them code

Every lesson has a 50-minute lesson plan aligned to the CSTA standards, slides to project before students start the puzzles, and a printable classwork sheet with an answer key. Free to use and adapt for your classes.

0. PlanRead the lesson plan: objectives, standards, timing, misconceptions and differentiation.
1. TeachOpen the slides and press F for full screen. Arrow keys move on, N shows teacher notes.
2. PracticePrint the classwork. "Print student pages" leaves out the answer key.
3. CodeStudents solve the lesson's 10 puzzles. Watch their stars live on the Teacher Dashboard.
