When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Face it first. The shell flies the way the rover faces.
  • Shells are limited. Only fire when a tank is in your line of fire.
  • Red ! means danger. Fire or move out of the tank's line on your next move.
  • Run, watch, fix. Count tanks and shells before you run.
Students who struggle with turns can stand up and turn with the rover.
