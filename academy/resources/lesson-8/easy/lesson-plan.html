Lesson 8: Tank practice

Time 50 minutes
Unit Loops and conditionals
Puzzles 8.1 to 8.10

Overview

A practice lesson with no new blocks. Students practice aiming: turning to face tanks in different directions, firing at tanks in a row with a repeat, and combining driving, turning and firing.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Choose the turn (left or right) that faces the rover toward a tank.
  2. Use a repeat to fire at several tanks in a row.
  3. Combine driving, turning and firing in one program.
  4. Use a fire-and-turn pattern inside a repeat.

Lesson procedure

TimeStageTeacher and student activity
5 minWarm-up
Point and shoot Slide 3		"Point and shoot": students turn and point in the directions the teacher calls.
10 minDirect instruction
Slides 4 to 6		Model aiming: turn until the rover faces the tank, then fire. Show a turn-around (puzzle 8.3) and a drive-then-fire pattern (puzzle 8.7).
5 minGuided practice
Think, pair, share Slides 7 to 8		Solve puzzle 8.7 together, then pairs solve puzzle 8.10 before the reveal.
12 minUnplugged practice
Classwork Slide 10		Students complete Parts A to C and E in pairs. Part C practices choosing turns.
15 minIndependent practice
Code Slide 11		Students solve puzzles 8.1 to 8.10. These are short; push for 3 stars.
3 minClosure
Exit ticket Slide 12		Exit ticket: rover faces right, tank above. Answer: L X.

Assessment

  • Formative: classwork Parts A to C, observation during coding, and the exit ticket.
  • Progress: the Teacher Dashboard shows stars per puzzle and a Lesson 8 score out of 100 (3 stars = 100, 2 stars = 90, 1 star = 80 per puzzle).
  • Success criteria: the student solves at least 9 of 10 puzzles and chooses the correct turn direction.

Common misconceptions

  • Using the screen's left/right instead of the rover's.
  • Thinking fire works in every direction at once.
  • Forgetting to turn back after firing in a repeated pattern.

Differentiation

  • Support: use a paper arrow students can rotate on the map.
  • Extension: 3 stars on all ten puzzles; challenge a partner with a Part D puzzle.
  • Accessibility: blocks can be added by tapping or by dragging, and moved with the ▲▼ buttons.

Supports for English learners

  • Visuals: post the block pictures and vocabulary on a word wall; point to blocks while you say them.
  • Gestures: students stand and turn left or right with the rover before coding turns.
  • Partners: pair students to read programs aloud; first-language talk is welcome.
  • Sentence frames: “The tank is behind the rover, so it turns ___ two times.” “First the rover ___. Then it fires.” “The shell hits the ___ tank in the line.”
  • Classwork Part E uses a word bank, pictures and fill-in sentences.

Connections

  • Math: quarter turns and angles (90°, 180°).
  • PE: facing and pointing drills.
