When it doesn't work

Debugging tips

  • Count tanks and shells before you build.
  • Watch the block counter: "6 of 10 max".
  • Find the pattern and put it in a repeat to save blocks.
  • Look before you fire when not every square has a tank.
If a student hits the limit, ask: which blocks are the same? Can a repeat hold them?
