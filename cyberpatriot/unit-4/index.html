Unit 4 of 10 · 2 weeks

Windows Hardening, Part 2: Services, Firewall, Sharing, Software, and Auditing

Finish the Windows core: decide which services to stop, turn on the firewall, close remote access, remove unsafe shares and prohibited software, and turn on auditing. After this unit, students can work a full Windows practice image on their own.

Time: 4 lessons (50 min) and 2 practice sessions (90 min)Platform: Windows 10/11
Goals

By the end of this unit, students can

  1. Explain what a service is and safely disable risky services the README does not require.
  2. Turn on Windows Defender Firewall and turn off Remote Desktop when it is not needed.
  3. Find and remove unsafe network shares, and set User Account Control and Microsoft Defender correctly.
  4. Remove prohibited software and turn on security auditing.
Signature lab

Lab 4: Full Windows Practice Image

A complete Windows image: forensics question, services, firewall and Remote Desktop, shares, UAC and Defender, prohibited software, and auditing. Students finish with a 50-minute timed run.

CSTA 3A-NI-06, 3B-NI-04 · NICE: Systems Administration, Cyber Defense Infrastructure Support
