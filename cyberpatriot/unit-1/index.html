CyberPatriot / Unit 1
Unit 1 of 10 · 2 weeks

Welcome to CyberPatriot and Cyber Ethics

Learn how a CyberPatriot round works, the CIA triad, and the legal and ethical limits of security work. Then open your first practice image and fix your first vulnerabilities.

Time: 4 lessons (50 min) and 2 practice sessions (90 min)Platform: Windows
Goals

By the end of this unit, students can

  1. Describe how a CyberPatriot round works: teams, images, the README, scoring, and penalties.
  2. Explain the CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity, availability) and give an example of each.
  3. Tell the difference between a threat, a vulnerability, and a risk.
  4. Explain the legal and ethical limits of security work and the competition code of conduct.
  5. Find and fix at least 3 basic vulnerabilities on a practice Windows virtual machine.
Signature lab

Lab 1: First Look at a Practice Image

In pairs, students read the README for a front-office Windows computer, answer the forensics question first, then check users, passwords, the firewall, and banned media files. The goal is at least 3 of the 8 planted problems in 60 minutes, then a 45-minute timed redo.

CSTA 3A-NI-05, 3A-NI-06, 3A-IC-24 · NICE: Cyber Defense Analysis
