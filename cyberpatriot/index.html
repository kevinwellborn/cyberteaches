How it works

What a week looks like

Two 50-minute lessons

Short teaching with the slides, a hands-on activity, and an exit ticket. Every lesson plan has a minute-by-minute script.

One 90-minute practice

Students work a lab on their own virtual machine, then redo it against a timer, just like a round.

A checklist that grows

Every unit adds tested checks and commands. By Unit 10 the team merges them into one master checklist for round day.

Ethics in every unit

Ethics is taught in Unit 1 and comes back as a 10-minute scenario discussion in every unit after that.

Getting ready

What you need

Equipment and software

  • Computers that can run virtual machines (8 GB RAM or more)
  • VirtualBox or VMware Workstation Player (free)
  • Windows 10/11 Enterprise and Windows Server evaluation images, and Ubuntu Desktop LTS
  • Cisco Packet Tracer (free with a Cisco Networking Academy account)
  • Official CyberPatriot practice images and training (for registered teams)

Team roles

Teams have up to 6 competitors (5 compete in a round) plus a coach. In class, rotate so every student practices each operating system, then specialize in Unit 10:

  • Windows lead
  • Linux lead
  • Windows Server lead
  • Cisco and networking lead
  • Captain: forensics questions, checklist, and the clock
Practice responsiblyEvery lab runs on virtual machines and simulators you set up yourself. Never scan, change, or test a computer or network you do not own or have written permission to use.
