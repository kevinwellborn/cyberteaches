CyberPatriot / Unit 9
Unit 9 of 10 · 3 weeks

Advanced Hardening and Forensics Questions

Forensics questions are often worth more than single fixes, and later rounds hide backdoors and scheduled tasks. Learn hashing and decoding, finding hidden files, removing persistence, tracing listening ports, reading logs, and spotting dangerous SUID files.

Time: 6 lessons (50 min) and 3 practice sessions (90 min)Platform: Ubuntu and Windows
Goals

By the end of this unit, students can

  1. Answer forensics questions without destroying evidence, including hashing and decoding questions.
  2. Find hidden, large, recently changed, and oddly owned files on Linux and Windows.
  3. Find and remove persistence: cron jobs, systemd services, scheduled tasks, and Run keys.
  4. Trace a listening port back to its program and file, and check the hosts file.
  5. Read sign-in logs and find dangerous SUID files.
Signature lab

Lab 9: Forensics Question Challenge Set

Ten items on an Ubuntu VM: five evidence questions (hashes, decoding, hidden files), then a cron job, a listening service, the hosts file, a log question, and a SUID file. Everything planted is harmless, but treat the VM as a lab machine only. The last run is timed at 55 minutes.
