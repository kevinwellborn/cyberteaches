CyberPatriot / Unit 10
Unit 10 of 10 · 3, then weekly weeks

Competition Strategy and Team Play

Strong teams are not the ones who know the most commands. They read carefully, split the work, and avoid penalties. Learn team roles, round-day logistics, prioritizing by points and time, using the scoring report, and building one team master checklist.

Time: 6 lessons (50 min) and 3 practice sessions (90 min), then a mock round every weekPlatform: All images and Cisco
Goals

By the end of this unit, students can

  1. Work in defined roles and communicate progress during a round.
  2. Prepare for round day: equipment, image setup, rules, and timing.
  3. Prioritize the work by points and time, and know when to move on.
  4. Use the scoring report to confirm fixes and catch penalties fast.
  5. Build and maintain a team master checklist from Units 1 to 9.
Signature lab

Lab 10: Full Mock Rounds as a Team

Teams run mock rounds on the Unit 4, 6, and 8 lab VMs and the Unit 7 Packet Tracer challenge, with assigned roles and a projector timer. The run sheet keeps the round on schedule, and every round ends with a debrief. No new setup is needed.

CSTA 3B-IC-27, 3A-AP-23 · NICE: Cyber Defense Analysis, Systems Administration
