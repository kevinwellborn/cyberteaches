Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this a complete homeschool computer science curriculum?

Yes. Together the courses cover middle school coding through two full AP courses, and each one has lessons, practice, labs or puzzles, and scored checks.

Do I need to know how to code to teach it?

No. The lessons explain each idea to your student directly, the puzzles and quizzes check the answers, and teacher resources include answer keys.

What ages is it for?

Middle and high school students, roughly ages 11 to 18. Beginners of any age can start with Rover Coding on the Easy level.

Can it count as a high school credit?

Many families count a full course, such as AP Computer Science Principles or AP Cybersecurity, as one computer science credit. Check the homeschool rules in your state.

Can homeschool students take the AP exam?

Yes. Homeschool students can take AP Exams at a school that offers them. Contact a local school's AP coordinator early in the school year to arrange it.

Does my child need an email address?

No. They can sign in with Google, or you can create a username and password for them in the dashboard.
