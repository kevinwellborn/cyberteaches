AP Computer Science Principles Practice Exam 1

A full-length practice exam in the same format as the AP CSP end-of-course exam. Every question is original and written for CyberTeaches.

SectionQuestionsTimeWhat it looks like
I: Multiple choice702 hoursQuestions 1–57 have one best answer. Questions 58–62 are about a reading passage on a computing innovation. Questions 131–138 ask you to select two answers. Code appears in text-based and block-based form, and some questions use a robot in a grid.
II: Written response2 questions (4 prompts)1 hourWritten Response 1 (program design, function, and purpose) and Written Response 2 parts (a) algorithm development, (b) errors and testing, and (c) data and procedural abstraction. You answer them about a sample program.

Coverage: Big Idea 1: 7 questions · Big Idea 2: 14 · Big Idea 3: 24 · Big Idea 4: 8 · Big Idea 5: 17, close to the official exam weighting. Every question links to the course topic to review.

Before you start

  • Open the Reference button during the exam to see the programming reference sheet, just like on test day. You can also view or print it now.
  • Have scratch paper ready. Calculators are not allowed on the AP CSP exam and are not needed.
  • Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the tab and resume later on the same device.
  • Each section has its own timer. When time runs out, the section is submitted automatically.
  • After Section II, you score your own written responses with the scoring guidelines and sample responses.
Prefer paper? Print the exam booklet (answer sheet, reference sheet, all 70 questions, and the written response section). Teachers can add the answer key at the end.
