THE CONGRESSIONAL AWARD

Award Tracker website privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Award Tracker is an independent tool, made by Kevin Wellborn, that helps students track their progress toward The Congressional Award. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Congressional Award Foundation. This policy covers the website at cyberteaches.com/award-tracker. The iPhone app has its own policy: it stores everything on the phone and collects nothing.

What the website stores

When you create an account, we store:

  • your sign-in details: your email address and name, through Google sign-in or an email and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us);
  • what you enter in the tracker: your name, Activity Start Date, optional birthday and Advisor name, your logged hours, goals, notes, trips, and any validator names and contact details you type in;
  • the email addresses of the Advisors you choose to share with.

We use this only to show you your records on any device you sign in on, and to show them to the Advisors you choose. We do not sell or rent it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. The website has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who can see your records

  • You. Only you can add, change or delete your entries.
  • Advisors you share with. On the Me tab you can enter an Advisor's email. That person can then sign in with that email (it must be verified) and see your progress, activity log and Record Book summary, read-only. You can stop sharing at any time, and they lose access right away.
  • Nobody else. Other students, other Advisors and visitors cannot see your records. Security rules on the database enforce this.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

Records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States and are sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The website does not keep a copy of your records on the computer after you sign out or close the tab. On a shared or school computer, tick "This is a shared or school computer" when you sign in, and sign out when you finish.

Deleting your data

  • Deleted entries go to "Recently deleted" on the Me tab for 30 days, where you can restore them, and are then removed for good.
  • "Delete my account" on the Me tab permanently deletes your account, all your entries and your Advisor sharing. Download a copy first if you want to keep one.
  • You can download a copy of your records at any time with "Download a copy".

Students under 18

The Congressional Award is for young people aged 13½ to 24, so the website is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, please use the website with your parent's or guardian's knowledge. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a student's account by emailing the address below.
