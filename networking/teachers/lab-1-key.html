Lab 1 Teacher Key: The Case of the Sluggish Device

Quick-fix results, the follow-up readings to hand out after each team implements a fix, the expected root causes, and the AI-evaluation key for all four case cards.

Setup and pacing

  • Before class: print one case card per team (or post them) and print the follow-up readings below on separate slips. Hand a slip only when a team tells you the exact solution it implemented.
  • Day 1: Parts 1–2. Day 2: Parts 3–4. Day 3: Part 5 (record or build slides). Walkthroughs can be finished as homework.
  • Assign cases so that neighboring teams have different ones. Case D is the hardest (two causes); give it to a strong team.
  • If a team implements a solution that does not match its cause, hand it the "no change" slip for that case. That is a teaching moment for 1.1.A.6 (the root cause might have been misidentified).

Case A: runaway process (high CPU)

ItemKey
Quick fix result (restart)"After the restart, the laptop is fine for about 10 minutes. Then CPU climbs back to 95%, and CloudSync Helper shows Not responding again. It is listed in Startup apps as Enabled."
Root causeOverloaded processor: CPU 96%, above 90% for 20 minutes; CloudSync Helper is using 78% and not responding. RAM (62%), storage (55% free), Wi-Fi (4 bars, 0% loss, 18 ms), and temperature are normal.
Best first solutionEnd the unresponsive CloudSync Helper process in Task Manager. Because it restarts at startup, also update the app or disable it in Startup apps.
Follow-up slip (correct fix)"CPU 14%. Video plays without buffering. CloudSync Helper updated to the newest version and no longer appears as not responding after two restarts."
AI suggestions1 reject (age is not supported; one process causes the load) · 2 keep · 3 reject (RAM is 62%, normal; costly) · 4 keep or test first (explains the return after restart)

Case B: low storage

ItemKey
Quick fix result (restart)"No change. Still 4.8 GB free, and the slideshow still will not save."
Root causeInsufficient storage: 4.8 ÷ 128 = 3.75% free, below 10%, with save errors and slow file access. CPU 22%, RAM 48%, and ping (0% loss, 24 ms) are normal.
Ranked solutionsDelete temporary files (9.2 GB) and old installers in Downloads; uninstall the two unused games (22.0 GB); move photos and videos to cloud or external storage; install more storage only if the problem persists.
Follow-up slip (temp files + unused games removed)"36.0 GB free of 128 GB. The slideshow saves, and files open in about one second." (4.8 + 9.2 + 22.0 = 36.0 GB; 36.0 ÷ 128 ≈ 28% free.)
Follow-up slip (temp files only)"14.0 GB free. The slideshow saves, but the low-space warning appears again after one large download." (14.0 ÷ 128 ≈ 10.9%, barely above the threshold, so a good team should keep going.)
AI suggestions1 keep · 2 reject (CPU is 22%) · 3 keep · 4 reject (disruptive and risks data loss; simpler fixes come first)

Case C: weak wireless signal

ItemKey
Quick fix result (restart the router or reconnect)"No change in the bedroom: still 1 bar, about 150 ms, 9% loss."
Root causeWeak wireless signal from distance and obstacles (two walls and a stairwell): 1 of 4 bars, 164 ms, 10% loss in the bedroom. The kitchen test (0% loss, 16 ms, 240 Mbps) shows the router and internet are fine. Bandwidth is not the cause: about 15 Mbps of a 300 Mbps plan is in use.
Best solutionConnect to the bedroom's Ethernet wall port (wired connection). Alternatives: move closer to the router or reduce obstacles.
Follow-up slip (Ethernet)"Wired: ping 0% loss, average 12 ms; speed test 285 Mbps. The show streams without buffering."
AI suggestions1 test first (simple, but the kitchen test already shows the router works; result: no change) · 2 reject (plenty of bandwidth; costly) · 3 keep · 4 keep

Case D: overheating plus high memory (two causes)

ItemKey
Quick fix resultThe laptop was already restarted after the shutdown. "Still hot, still slow."
Root causes(1) Overheating: blanket blocks vents, direct sunlight, 97 °C, speed reduced to 0.9 GHz, protective shutdown at 1:58 p.m. (2) High RAM use: 91%, above 85% for 30 minutes, with 38 browser tabs using 4.6 GB.
Best solutionsMove the laptop to a hard, flat surface away from the window (ventilation, cooler area); close unused tabs and the idle video call app. Install more RAM only if use stays high afterward.
Follow-up slip (both fixes)"CPU temperature 62 °C, processor speed 2.6 GHz, RAM 58%. No shutdowns in the last hour."
Follow-up slip (cooling only)"Temperature 64 °C and no shutdowns, but pages still lag; RAM 90%."
Follow-up slip (closing tabs only)"RAM 57%, but the temperature warning returns after 15 minutes and the speed drops to 1.1 GHz."
AI suggestions1 reject (no evidence of malware; three antivirus programs conflict and add load) · 2 keep · 3 keep · 4 test first (only if RAM stays high)

Scoring notes

  • Evidence (4 pts): full credit needs the reading and the threshold, such as "3.75% free, below 10%." Saying "storage is low" earns 2.
  • Verification (3 pts): a team that got a "no change" slip and documented what it did next can earn full credit. That is the process working.
  • Prevention (2 pts): good security habits include automatic updates (1.4.B.3), unique passwords with MFA (1.4.B.4, 1.4.B.6), and disabling automatic connection to open networks.
  • Common mistakes: blaming the internet in Case A (the ping is fine); choosing "buy more storage" first in Case B; choosing a faster plan in Case C; finding only one of the two causes in Case D.

Exit ticket (optional, 5 min)

"A device shows 92% RAM for an hour and 6% free storage. Which problem would you address first, and why?" Accept either order with a justification that ranks solutions by simplicity and disruption (for example, closing apps first because it is instant and free, then deleting temporary files).
