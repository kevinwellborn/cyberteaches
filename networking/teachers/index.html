Fitting the labs into the pacing calendar

The book's pacing calendar gives each unit's review week 5–6 periods for the unit review, the lab, and the unit test. Labs 1 and 2 fit in that week. For Labs 3 and 4 (5 periods each), start the early parts alongside the last lessons of the unit: Lab 3 Parts 1–3 during Lessons 3.1–3.4, and Lab 4 Parts 1–2 during Lesson 4.3. Finish the build and the deliverable in the review week.

Team roles

Every lab uses the same four roles so that students learn them once: project lead, data analyst, configuration technician, and documentation lead. Rotate roles from lab to lab. The team log in each Word template records each role's contribution and how the team used AI, which is the evidence for skill category 4 (Collaborate).

About the practice exam format

The AP Networking exam format has not been published. The practice exams use the published AP Cybersecurity structure (60 MCQ in 80 minutes for 70%, 1 multi-source FRQ in 50 minutes for 30%) and weight units by the book's class periods. Every question is original and tagged to a framework learning objective and skill. The engine's weighted score is for practice only; there is no official score conversion.
