LAB 3 - AI-GENERATED DESIGN DRAFT                cyberteaches.com/networking/lab-3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A volunteer typed this prompt into an AI tool:
  "Design a network for a school esports tournament."
The AI replied:

  Recommended design
  1. Topology: a full mesh, with every switch and WAP cabled to every other
     switch and WAP so nothing can ever fail.
  2. Wireless: 2 high-power WAPs in the gym cover everyone. Put the 20 player
     PCs on Wi-Fi to avoid running cables across the stage.
  3. One SSID, "Esports-Event", with a password announced over the speakers
     so that players, coaches, judges, staff, and fans can all connect easily.
  4. Subnets:
       Players     10.60.0.0/26
       Production  10.60.0.32/27
       Staff       10.60.0.128/25
  5. Security: install a next-generation firewall at the perimeter and allow
     all traffic inside the event network so that nothing gets blocked during
     matches.

Your task: evaluate each numbered item. Which parts are workable, which are
overcomplicated or too costly, which miss a requirement in the event brief,
and which are technically wrong? Justify every judgment with the brief.
