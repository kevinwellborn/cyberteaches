Unit labs

Each lab is built on the unit scenario in the AP Networking Course Framework. Teams of four take defined roles, work with real-looking evidence and a simulator, evaluate AI output, and produce a professional deliverable scored with a published rubric.

Unit 1

Lab 1: The Case of the Sluggish Device

Diagnose a slow laptop from CPU, RAM, storage, Wi-Fi, and ping readings, then record a device recovery walkthrough.

Unit 2

Lab 2: Computer Lab Disconnected

Work six help desk tickets from indicator lights, ipconfig, and ping; document the room's network; secure and segment it.

Lab 3: Game Day Network Design

Practice responsibly

The labs use simulators, classroom lab equipment, and files from fictional organizations. Never run a scan, change a configuration, or test a network you do not own or have written permission to use.
