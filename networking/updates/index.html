AP Networking exam format

Status as of November 2026

The College Board describes the AP Networking Exam as a digital exam with two sections: multiple-choice questions connected to real-world job scenarios, and free-response questions that use real networking artifacts and evidence. It has not yet published the number of questions, the timing, the section weights, or the score scale.

Until it does, the two practice exams on this site follow the published structure of AP Cybersecurity, the sister AP Career Kickstart course. As soon as the College Board releases the AP Networking format, the exams will be updated and the changes will be listed here. Check AP Central for official information.
