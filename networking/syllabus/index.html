AP Networking Sample Syllabus

An editable Word syllabus organized so that a reviewer can find each part of your course quickly. The book's pacing calendar, learning objectives, and page numbers are already filled in. Replace everything in [brackets] with your school's details.

Before you submit a syllabus

AP Networking is a College Board pilot course. Check AP Central for the current AP Course Audit process and requirements for AP Networking, and compare your syllabus with the current course framework. This template is a starting point, not an approved syllabus.
