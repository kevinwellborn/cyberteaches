Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn cybersecurity with no experience?

Yes. AP Cybersecurity starts from zero with everyday attacks like phishing and weak passwords, and builds up to networks, devices and cryptography.

Is the Security+ course up to date?

Yes. It follows the CompTIA Security+ SY0-801 exam objectives across all five domains, including the AI and zero trust topics.

Is it free?

The courses, labs and practice questions are free. The CompTIA Security+ exam itself is paid and booked through CompTIA.

Who is it for?

High school students, college students, career changers and anyone who wants to understand how to stay safe online or start an IT or cybersecurity career.
