AP Cybersecurity practice exams

Two full-length practice exams in the format of the AP Cybersecurity exam: 60 multiple-choice questions in 80 minutes (70% of the score), then one Device Security Analysis free-response question in 50 minutes (30%). Every question, log, and policy is original.

Practice Exam 1

Exam 1

Multiple-choice sets on a phishing email, badge logs, firewall rules, an SSH log, SQL injection, and file integrity. Free response: a yearbook club photo server (5 parts, 11 points).
