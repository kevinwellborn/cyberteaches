CyberTeaches

Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026. CyberTeaches (cyberteaches.com) is run by teacher Kevin Wellborn.

The short version
  • All lessons are free and you never need an account to use them.
  • We only email you if you sign up yourself, and you must be 13 or older to sign up.
  • We never sell or rent your information, and we never send marketing to CyberTeaches Academy students.

What we collect

  • Nothing, if you just use the site. Progress is saved in your own browser.
  • Optional sign-in. If you sign in with Google to save progress, we store your name, email address, and course progress.
  • Email list (opt-in). If you sign up for exam tips, we store your email address, the time you signed up, the page you signed up on, and the fact that you confirmed you are 13 or older and agreed to get emails.
  • Teacher accounts. Teachers who ask for access give their name, email, and school so we can approve them.
  • CyberTeaches Academy (grades 6 to 8). Teachers can create student logins with a name and username. These logins have no real email address. Academy progress is visible only to the student and their teacher.

How we use it

  • To save your progress and show teachers their class results.
  • If you joined the email list: to send AP exam tips, new practice tests, study plans, and news about Kevin Wellborn's study guides. A few emails a month at most.
  • We do not use course progress or student information for advertising. Signing in never adds you to the email list.

Children and students

The email list is only for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect email addresses from children under 13 for marketing. CyberTeaches Academy users never see the sign-up and are never emailed. If you think a child under 13 joined the list, contact us and we will delete it.

Unsubscribing and deleting your information

Every email has an unsubscribe link and our mailing address. To be removed from the list, or to have your account and progress deleted, email [contact email to be added] and we will do it within 30 days.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores sign-ins, progress, and the email list.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Google Fonts provides the fonts.
  • YouTube plays embedded lesson videos, under YouTube's own privacy policy.
  • We may use an email service (such as MailerLite or Mailchimp) to send list emails. It only receives the addresses of people who signed up.

Amazon affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate, CyberTeaches earns from qualifying purchases. Links to books on Amazon may include our affiliate tag. Amazon does not share your name or purchases with us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
