App Builder topic C.5

Make the official demo video

The rules call the demonstration video the most critical part of your application. Judges may never run your app. They learn about it from your video. It must be 1 to 3 minutes long, cover six required things, and be uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo with the visibility set to public.

The six required things are: the name of each participant, the name of the app, the purpose of the app in one clear sentence, the target audience, the tools and coding languages used, and a showcase of the app's functionality. Write a script that checks off each one, and spend most of the time showing the app working.

The rules say this is not a video-creation competition, so skip fancy effects. Do make it clear and compelling. Stay inside the time limit, because videos that are too short or too long may be penalized. Lesson 6.3 covers how to record. This lesson is about getting the content right.

Script that covers all six

1. Names:      "Hi, I'm Maya, and this is my teammate Leo."
2. App name:   "Our app is called Homework Planner."
3. Purpose:    "It helps students see what is due next so that nothing gets forgotten."
4. Audience:   "We built it for middle school students who juggle many classes."
5. Tools:      "We wrote it in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, using a browser and GitHub."
6. Show it:    Add an assignment, show the sorted list, mark one done, reload to show it saves.

Close: one thing we learned, and what we would add next.

Do this

  1. Write your script in the box below. Cover all six required items.
  2. Read it aloud with a timer. Aim for about two minutes, and never more than three.
  3. Record it using the steps in lesson 6.3.
  4. Watch it with the checklist in the example. Fix anything missing and record again if needed.
  5. With a parent or guardian's OK, upload it to YouTube or Vimeo and set it to public.
  6. Open the link in a private browser window to make sure anyone can watch it. Save the link.

Make the official demo video vocabulary

  • Demonstration video: The 1 to 3 minute video that explains and shows your app. The most important part of your entry.
  • Functionality: What your app actually does when someone uses it.
  • Target audience: The specific people your app is made for.
  • Public: A video setting that lets anyone watch. Required by the rules.
  • Penalized: Scored lower for breaking a rule.
  • Script: A written plan of what you will say and show.

Quick check questions

Try each one, then open the answer. To save your progress, take this quiz in the interactive course.

1. How long must the demonstration video be?

  1. (A) Under 30 seconds
  2. (B) 1 to 3 minutes
  3. (C) Exactly 5 minutes
  4. (D) Any length
Show answer

(B) 1 to 3 minutes The rules require 1 to 3 minutes. Videos outside that range may be penalized.

2. Which of these is NOT one of the six required items?

  1. (A) The app's purpose in one sentence
  2. (B) The tools and coding languages used
  3. (C) The number of lines of code
  4. (D) The target audience
Show answer

(C) The number of lines of code The six items are names, app name, purpose, audience, tools and languages, and functionality.

3. What visibility setting must the uploaded video have?

  1. (A) Private
  2. (B) Public
  3. (C) Friends only
  4. (D) Deleted after a week
Show answer

(B) Public The rules say the video must be set to public.
