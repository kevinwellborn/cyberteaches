Congressional Award Tracker

Log your Congressional Award hours, see how close you are to each Certificate and Medal, and share your progress with your Advisor.

Open the tracker on the web Get the iPhone app

Made by teacher Kevin Wellborn. Not made or endorsed by The Congressional Award.

What you can log

All four Congressional Award program areas

Add hours after you finish an activity. The tracker adds them up and shows your progress toward the Bronze, Silver and Gold Certificates and Medals.

Voluntary Public Service

Volunteer hours that help your community, including planning and meetings.

Personal Development

Hours spent building a skill, hobby or interest you want to grow in.

Physical Fitness

Hours working toward a fitness goal, from team sports to running or swimming.

Expedition or Exploration

The days and nights of your trip, with the planning that goes into it.

How it works

Three steps to stay on track

1

Sign in

Create a free account on the website or the iPhone app. Use the same account on both and your entries sync.

2

Log your hours

Pick the program area, then add the date, hours, the goal they count toward, notes and your validator.

3

See your progress

Watch each Certificate and Medal fill up, then make a PDF of every entry that earned a level.

For Advisors

See your students' progress

A student enters your email to share with you. Sign in with that email to see their progress, activity log and Record Book summary. Only the student can change their entries, and they can stop sharing at any time.

For schools

Add it to your Clever Portal

Schools can add the tracker as a link so students open it from their Clever page.

  1. In Clever, choose to add a link (admins on the Portal, teachers on their Teacher Page).
  2. Paste this address: https://cyberteaches.com/award-tracker/
  3. Name it "Congressional Award Tracker" and upload this icon.
Questions

Congressional Award Tracker FAQ

Is the Congressional Award Tracker free?

Yes. The tracker is free on the web and as an iPhone app.

Is this the official Congressional Award website?

No. Award Tracker is made by Kevin Wellborn and is not made or endorsed by The Congressional Award. You register for the program and submit your official Record Book at congressionalaward.org. The tracker is your own record that helps you get ready.

What can I log in the tracker?

Hours for the four program areas: Voluntary Public Service, Personal Development, Physical Fitness, and Expedition or Exploration. Each entry keeps the date, hours, goal, notes and validator.

Can my Advisor see my progress?

Yes. Enter your Advisor's email and they can sign in to see your progress, activity log and Record Book summary. They cannot change your entries, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Does it work on a phone and a computer?

Yes. Your entries are saved to your account, so the website and the iPhone app show the same records when you sign in with the same account.

How is my information protected?

Read the Award Tracker privacy policy. On a shared or school computer, always sign out when you finish.
